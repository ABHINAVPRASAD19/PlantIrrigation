Wiring Connections Table

ESP32 Pin 2-Channel Relay Component Controlled
GPIO 32 (D32) IN1 (Input 1) Pump 1 (Plant 1: Mint)
GPIO 33 (D33) IN2 (Input 2) Pump 2 (Plant 2: Thyme)
GND GND • Common Rail CRITICAL: Common Ground
5V / VIN VCC Relay logic coil supply
⚠️ Power Isolation Rule:

Do NOT connect the submersible pumps directly to the ESP32's 5V pin! Power the pumps via an external 5V power bank or adapter connected through the Relay's COM and NO contacts.
